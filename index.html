Where science meets self-care

A med spa run like a clinic, with the warmth of a retreat.

Injected Artistry was founded by registered nurse Jamie Slovenski and physician Dr. Ken Starr to bring genuine medical standards to aesthetics and wellness on the Monterey Peninsula. Every treatment is delivered by a licensed RN, under medical direction, in a space designed to feel calm and private.

From a single Botox visit to a full IV hydration ritual, you are cared for by people who treat your safety and your results with the same attention.
